# 📏🥤 Measurement Hero Adventure

An interactive browser-based educational game that teaches **Length, Distance, and Liquid Measurement** to **Class 3 (8–9 years old)** students through fun, real-life adventures.

Children join **Bobo the Funny Robot 🤖**, who always gets measurements wrong! Players help Bobo by choosing the correct measurement unit or quantity, learning naturally through play, laughter, and exploration.

---

# 🎯 Learning Objectives

By playing this game, students will learn to:

- Understand different units of **Length and Distance**
    - Millimetre (mm)
    - Centimetre (cm)
    - Metre (m)
    - Kilometre (km)

- Understand different units of **Capacity (Liquid Measurement)**
    - Millilitre (ml)
    - Litre (L)

- Choose appropriate units for everyday objects and situations.

- Estimate reasonable measurements.

- Connect mathematics with daily life.

- Improve logical thinking and decision-making.

# 🎮 Story

Professor Bubble built the world's funniest robot named **Bobo**.

Unfortunately...

Bobo doesn't understand measurements.

Sometimes he

😂 drives **500 km** to visit the bathroom.

😂 pours **20 litres** of juice into a tiny glass.

Your job is to help Bobo make the correct choices and become the smartest delivery robot in the city!

# 🎲 Gameplay

Each round

1. Bobo appears with a funny mistake.
2. A random real-life scenario is generated.
3. Four answer options appear.
4. The player selects the best answer.
5. Immediate feedback is shown.
6. Bobo celebrates (or makes a funny mistake).
7. A brand-new random question is generated.

The game can continue indefinitely.

# 🧠 Educational Philosophy

Children learn best when mathematics is connected to their everyday life.

Instead of memorizing measurement units, this game encourages children to make decisions in familiar situations—measuring a pencil, filling a glass, watering a plant, or estimating the distance to school. Humorous mistakes by Bobo create memorable learning moments and help reinforce correct concepts through play.
